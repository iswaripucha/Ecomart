EcoMart Selling Policies

Please read the following policies carefully before listing your items on EcoMart.

Important Alert! ×

All items listed must be owned by the seller and in good, usable condition. Any violation of these rules may lead to removal of the listing, temporary suspension, or a fine up to 10% of the item’s price.

Promote Sustainability!

EcoMart encourages students to reuse and recycle items within the campus. Instead of discarding items, list them to sell or donate to fellow students, reducing waste and promoting eco-friendly habits.

Sell Your Items & Help Fellow Students! ♻️

Have unused or gently used items like books, gadgets, or clothing? Post them on EcoMart to earn money or donate. EcoMart makes selling simple, safe, and convenient.

Pricing Guidelines

  • Price your items fairly and transparently to ensure smooth transactions.
  • Donations are welcomed for items you no longer need.

Why Sell on EcoMart?

  • Support Sustainability – Reduce campus waste and reuse items.
  • Easy Process – Simple form to list items quickly.
  • Community Engagement – Help other students access affordable items.
  • Secure Platform – Only verified college students can list and buy items.

How It Works?

  • Fill the Form – Provide item details, photos, and price.
  • Get Connected – Buyers can contact you through EcoMart.
  • Donate Option – Mark items for donation if you don’t want to sell.

Items Not Allowed

Do not list the following items:

  • Hazardous or illegal items
  • Expired or perishable goods
  • Offensive or inappropriate materials
  • Broken or unusable items
  • Items violating college rules or policies

List your items today, earn money, or donate, and help make your campus more sustainable! ♻️
