About EcoMart

EcoMart is a web-based platform developed by IT engineering students to promote sustainability and cost-effectiveness within the college campus.

It encourages students to reuse, recycle, and resell items instead of discarding them, supporting a circular and eco-friendly economy in education.

The platform allows students to buy, sell, or donate reusable items such as books, electronics, furniture, and clothes — making campus life more affordable, sustainable, and cost-effective while reducing waste generation.
